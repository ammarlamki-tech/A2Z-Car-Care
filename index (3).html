FAQ

Common questions

How do I get a price for my car?

Use the quote tool above or message us directly on WhatsApp with your car's make, model and the service you're interested in. We'll reply with pricing and scheduling.

Do you offer full-body or partial PPF?

Both. We install full-body, partial, and custom PPF packages in matte or clear finish, depending on the level of coverage you want.

What's the difference between PPF and ceramic coating?

PPF is a physical film that absorbs impacts like stone chips and scuffs. Ceramic coating is a chemical sealant that adds gloss and a hydrophobic surface but doesn't add impact protection. Many customers combine both — ask us what fits your car.

Can dents really be fixed without repainting?

Yes — our SmartDENT process massages the panel back into shape from behind, so your original factory paint stays untouched.

How long does an installation take?

It depends on your vehicle and the service — a full PPF wrap takes longer than a partial tint job, for example. Send us your car details on WhatsApp and we'll give you an accurate timeframe.

Where are you located?

We're based in Ghala, Muscat. See the map and directions in the Location section below.
